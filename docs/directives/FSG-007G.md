# FSG-007G — Homepage Product Maturity Reconciliation

## Status

CURRENT — implementation authorized; Product Office visual approval pending.

## Parent Milestone

FSG-007 — Public Product Experience & Launch

## Governing Directive

FSG-007A — Complete Front-Facing Website Redesign

## Objective

Bring the public homepage up to the product that now exists. The homepage must
present File. Set. Go. as a file-readiness product with three primary work
modes: Quick Fit, Guided Fit and Logo Pack.

The approved central proposition remains:

> Get your file ready for where it needs to go.

## Presentation Contract

The homepage remains the primary product entrance. Its visual and narrative
architecture must connect the proposition directly to the live FILE / SET / GO
workspace, then explain:

- the three work modes;
- destination-aware Hero, Content and Card preparation;
- user-approved crop and focus;
- the governed FILE → SET → GO processing system;
- validated result truth;
- browser-local preparation; and
- practical acquisition-task paths.

The homepage must not become an SEO article, a generic SaaS shell or a second
copy of any acquisition page.

## Frozen Product Boundary

This is a presentation milestone. It does not authorize changes to Quick Fit,
Guided Fit, Logo Pack, unified source handling, crop behavior, enlargement
approval, processing phases, worker behavior, source gating, result truth or
deep-link contracts.

FSG-007F remains independently awaiting Product Office visual approval. This
milestone may reuse its governed assets without changing its page composition
or status.

## Verification Gate

Implementation requires focused homepage coverage for workspace boot, source
state, mode tabs and launchers, source-required gating, truthful destination
values, current processing grammar, task routes, dark mode, 320/390/430
responsive behavior and the product-family footer.

Automated screenshot evidence must cover the empty and selected workspace,
all three launch modes, destination and crop stories, processing, trust, task
directory, light and dark themes, mobile, tablet and footer states.

## Stop Condition

Do not mark FSG-007G complete until Product Office reviews the rendered
implementation. Do not begin final FSG-007 launch certification, deploy, or
begin FSG-008 from this milestone.
