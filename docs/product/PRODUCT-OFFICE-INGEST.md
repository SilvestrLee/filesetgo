## Governance Rule

The full handover is preserved so no source information is lost.

Derived documents distinguish canonical/accepted principles from working recommendations. They do not automatically override:

1. `docs/governance/MASTER-BLUEPRINT.md`
2. `docs/governance/DECISIONS.md`
3. `docs/governance/ROADMAP.md`

The next controlled repository step should cross-reference these documents from the existing Product Definition / Master Blueprint and selectively record binding decisions in `DECISIONS.md` after FSG-007 lineage reconciliation.
