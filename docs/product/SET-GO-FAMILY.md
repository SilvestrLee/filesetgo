# Set. Go. Product Family

## Family Philosophy

The strongest umbrella idea is:

> Creation tools help people make things. Set. Go. products help people make those things ready for the real world.

Each product should remain independently useful. Cross-promotion is encouraged when contextual, but technical dependency is not.

## File. Set. Go.

Purpose:

> Get files ready.

Current product.

## Site. Set. Go.

Recommended next product after FileSetGo.

Purpose:

> Get websites ready.

Concept:

A URL-based launch-readiness checker that may inspect issues such as oversized images, favicons, logo preparation, broken links, alt text, Open Graph, metadata, accidental noindex, 404 behavior, mobile issues, forms, analytics, privacy/cookies, HTTPS/security basics, sitemap/robots, performance, accessibility basics, and general launch readiness.

Natural cross-promotion example:

> Oversized hero detected → Fix with File. Set. Go.

## Brand. Set. Go.

Recommended positioning:

> Make an existing brand usable everywhere.

Avoid reducing the concept to another AI logo generator.

Future possibilities include:

- logo variants;
- transparent versions;
- light/dark versions;
- favicon;
- social avatars;
- safe-area guidance;
- brand colours;
- typography;
- email signature assets;
- print/web assets;
- basic brand guide;
- downloadable Brand Pack.

## Shop. Set. Go.

Potentially broad commercial surface, but significantly more complex.

Possible readiness areas:

- product imagery;
- descriptions;
- variations;
- pricing;
- payments;
- shipping;
- taxes;
- policies;
- categories;
- merchandising;
- search;
- SEO;
- social sharing;
- abandoned carts;
- email;
- inventory;
- fulfilment;
- notifications.

Recommendation: build after more readiness-product experience is gained.

## Working Family Order

Recommended, not irreversible:

> File. Set. Go. → Site. Set. Go. → Brand. Set. Go. → Shop. Set. Go.

## Cross-Promotion Rules

- never place sister-product promotion ahead of a user's current result/download;
- show at most the contextually relevant sister product where practical;
- unlaunched products display `Coming soon`;
- no fake links or destinations;
- future `Notify me` interest capture may be used to validate demand by sister product.
